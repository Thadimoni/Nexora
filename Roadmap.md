# Nexora Assessment Platform

## Vision

To build a modern, scalable, commercial assessment platform that can be used by:

- Schools
- Universities
- Training Centres
- Recruitment Agencies
- Government Examination Bodies
- WAEC Practice
- JAMB Practice
- Professional Certification Organizations
